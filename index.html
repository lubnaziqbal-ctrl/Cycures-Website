Risk What cybersecurity is

Protecting your company's digital house.

Cybersecurity protects a company's data, systems, people and money from digital thieves and from accidents.

Think of it this wayIf your company were a bank branch, cybersecurity would be its locks, CCTV, guards, alarm and emergency plan. The same protection, applied to computers, networks, cloud and data.
  • People training
  • Process policies
  • Technology tools
  • not just antivirus software
  • C

    Confidentiality

    Only the right people see the data.

    ExampleCustomer card numbers visible only to the payments team.

  • I

    Integrity

    Data is accurate and unaltered.

    ExampleNobody can secretly change a supplier's bank account number.

  • A

    Availability

    Systems work when they are needed.

    ExampleYour online store stays up on a sale day.

Together these three goals are called the CIA triad, the foundation of every security program.

Visibility VAPT

Find the weakness before they do.

A safe, controlled break-in test. Specialists combine automated and manual testing to find weaknesses in networks, servers, applications and websites before real attackers do. Management gets visibility into cybersecurity risk, a clear basis for prioritizing security investment and a lower likelihood of disruption and financial loss.

Findings rated by severity, exploitability and business impact

  • CRITICALAct immediately
  • HIGHFix as a priority
  • MEDIUMPlan remediation
  • LOWTrack and review

How findings get fixed

  • Security patches
  • Software upgrades
  • Configuration corrections
  • Removing unnecessary services
  • Fixing vulnerable code

RecommendationRun VAPT periodically and after significant infrastructure or application changes. Prioritize remediation by severity and business impact, and report critical findings, overdue remediation actions and residual risks to management.

Protection Governance, risk & compliance

Security needs governance.

GRC documentation is the collection of policies, procedures, registers, assessments, approvals, reports and evidence that shows an organization governs its activities, manages its risks and meets applicable requirements.

It sets the policies and processes that keep operations running smoothly, tracks document owners, versions, approvals and reviews, and keeps track of the laws and regulatory obligations that apply to you.

Create clear rules, ownership and accountability across your organization.

Policies

How GRC is structured

Governance → Risk → Controls → Assurance

  1. 01GovernanceDefine policies, responsibilities and business objectives.
  2. 02Risk assessmentIdentify, evaluate and prioritize risks.
  3. 03Controls and complianceImplement controls and map applicable requirements.
  4. 04Monitoring and assuranceConduct audits, assessments and control testing.

  • Internal audit complianceReviewing control effectiveness, identifying gaps and tracking corrective actions.

Compliance ISO 27001 & other certifications

Build trust into your security framework.

ISO 27001 is the international standard for managing information security. CYCURES helps organizations achieve and maintain ISO/IEC 27001:2022 compliance: assessing the current security posture, developing the required documentation, implementing controls, preparing for audits and managing information security risks. Certification itself is granted by an accredited certification body.
